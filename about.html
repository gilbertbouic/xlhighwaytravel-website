Our story

Durban roots. National reach.

“Ethics, transparency and honesty guide every booking.”

XL Highway Travel began in 1998 as a community travel agency in Durban’s Upper Highway area — first known as Harvey World Travel Highway. For more than two decades we have helped South African families, businesses and organisations travel with confidence.

Today we trade as New Heights CC 1208 T/A XL Highway Travel, a member of the XL Travel Group. That group strength gives our clients preferred rates with airlines, hotels, car hire, tours and cruises — while we keep the personal, owner-led service that Brigitte built our reputation on.

Our consultants work remotely across South Africa. You can reach us online, by phone or WhatsApp. Face-to-face meetings are by appointment only — efficient for you, flexible for us, without losing the human relationship.
