4. Compare alternate routes — rebook only in your GDS.

Traffic lights

What “delay decision” means

Each stop and connection is scored for congestion, buffer length and recovery options. You get a short plain-language reason for why that leg is green, amber or red — so you can explain the advice to the client with confidence.

Important

This tool advises only. Always confirm times and inventory in the airline/GDS before ticketing. Scope: scheduled passenger flights.
